White Water Rafting Co.

At White Water Rafting Co., we are passionate about providing thrilling and unforgettable rafting experiences. Our team of experienced guides is dedicated to ensuring your safety and enjoyment on the water. Whether you are a beginner or a seasoned pro, adventure awaits! Our Mission: To create unforgettable outdoor adventures that connect people with nature, build confidence, and spark a lifelong love for the river. Our Motto: “Ride the rapids. Find your wild.

History

White Water Rafting Co. was born in the summer of 1995, when three college friends armed with a used raft, a leaky cooler, and far too much enthusiasm took their first commercial trip down the river. They had no office, no website, and no idea what they were doing. What they did have was a belief that everyone deserves to feel the rush of the river at least once in their life. That first summer, they guided 47 guests. Today, we guide thousands each season. Along the way, we have earned a reputation for safety, professionalism, and unforgettable adventures. We have trained Olympic hopefuls, hosted corporate retreats, and introduced countless kids to their first taste of white water. The rafts have changed. The river has not. And neither has our reason for being here.
