Our Rafting Trips

From gentle floats to extreme white water — find the perfect river adventure.

Trip Highlights

Beginner Rapids — Half Day

Our most popular introduction to white water. Three hours of gentle Class I–II rapids, calm stretches for swimming, and a knowledgeable guide who knows every rock on the river. Ideal for families with kids aged six and up, first-timers, and anyone who wants scenery over adrenaline.

  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Includes: Raft, paddle, PFD, splash jacket, guide
  • Price: $70 per person

Mountain Run — Full Day

Five hours on the river through steady Class II–III rapids with a riverside lunch stop in the canyon. You will hit the famous Big Mallard rapid, float past towering cliffs, and finish with a stretch of calm water perfect for photos. Suitable for ages twelve and up with a moderate fitness level.

  • Difficulty: Medium
  • Duration: 5 hours
  • Includes: All gear, riverside lunch, shuttle
  • Price: $100 per person

Extreme Challenge — Los Dientes del Diablo

A full day of continuous Class IV white water for experienced paddlers only. This is the trip that made our river famous. Steep drops, technical boulder gardens and the legendary Los Dientes del Diablo rapid. Not for the faint of heart — prior rafting experience is required.

  • Difficulty: Hard
  • Duration: Full day
  • Includes: All gear, two meals, safety kayaker
  • Price: $200 per person
