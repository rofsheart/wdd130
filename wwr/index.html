Ride the Rapids. Find Your Wild.

Welcome to White Water Rafting Co. From gentle family floats to heart-pounding Class IV white water, we have an adventure for every paddler. Certified guides, top-of-the-line gear and unbeatable river knowledge make every trip safe, thrilling and unforgettable.

Our Purpose

White Water Rafting Co. exists to connect people with the river. We believe time on the water builds confidence, strengthens teams and creates memories that outlast any souvenir.

Today we guide thousands of guests each season — families on their first float, corporate groups chasing a shared challenge, and seasoned paddlers returning year after year for the next big rapid.
